# Behara / به‌آرا — calligraphic identity

The Persian name is the emblem. Broad diagonal strokes, circular counters, precise cuts and a compact silhouette follow the visual direction of the supplied references, with a new letter composition for Behara. Use the same emblem in Persian and English contexts; BEHARA is a supporting Latin signature.

Files: `behara-logo-v3.svg` (midnight), `behara-logo-v3-champagne.svg` (light), and `behara-calligraphic-v3.png` (transparent source). The SVG contains editable silhouette paths, not text or embedded fonts. The original concept was generated using the built-in ImageGen tool; the alpha silhouette was converted into SVG contours by `scripts/trace-brand.py`.

Use one color and preserve proportions. Leave clear space at least the diameter of the dot. Do not add a separate badge or stock jewelry icon. Minimum logo width: 64px for the full calligraphic name. Use midnight on champagne and champagne on midnight or moss. Keep the website palette and supporting editorial typography.

The previous leaf and circular seal concepts are retired. This is a visual identity concept; no trademark registration is implied.
